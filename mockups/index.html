Volts&Watts

Homepage mockups: three directions

Each mockup has every homepage section from the proposal: hero, fast-contact bar, core services, commercial, why us, recent work, service area and the estimate form. Each also has a sticky Call / Text / WhatsApp / Estimate bar on mobile. Photo slots are labeled placeholders until real job photos arrive.

A · Neon Noir

Neon Noir

Best for: brand fidelity

Uses the glowing-bulb logo as the full-bleed hero. Charcoal with an amber glow and Oswald headlines. This is the proposal's recommended "premium dark industrial" look, almost word for word.
B · Blueprint

Blueprint

Best for: winning commercial clients

A technical spec-sheet look with grid lines, condensed type and safety-gold accents. The commercial section comes right after the hero, and services are laid out as a spec table. It's built for facility managers and the recurring-maintenance growth goal.
C · Front Porch

Front Porch

Best for: homeowners & conversion

Light, warm and approachable, with the estimate form above the fold so it converts fastest. The dark logo becomes a badge, and the commercial section is a single dark band. It reads friendlier to homeowners but is less tied to the logo's mood.

All three are static HTML with no build step. Once a direction is picked, it gets rebuilt in Astro + Tailwind for Cloudflare Pages, as the proposal specifies. Anything in [brackets], such as license number, warranty terms or emergency hours, is waiting on confirmation from Volts & Watts.
